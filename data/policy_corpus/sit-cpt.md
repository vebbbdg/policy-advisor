##

Some F-1 students are eligible for practical training. There are two types of F-1 practical training: curricular practical training (CPT) and optional practical training (OPT). Designated school officials (DSOs) should understand the difference between CPT and OPT as well as their responsibilities to ensure proper CPT authorization.

Type of Practical Training Distinction

Curricular Practical Training (CPT)
- Alternative work/study, internship, cooperative education or any other type of required internship or practicum that is offered by sponsoring employers through cooperative agreements with the school.

- Is an integral part of an established curriculum.

- Relates directly to the student’s major area of study.

- Occurs before the student’s program end date on the Form I-20.

- Students must make an official request for CPT authorization to their DSO so the school can confirm that the student is participating in an established curriculum where practical training is integral and make a determination that the training is directly related to the student’s major area of study.

- If eligible, DSO authorizes CPT in SEVIS, and the authorization prints on the student’s Form I-20.

- CPT must be authorized before the student can begin work. The student cannot begin work before the CPT start date indicated on the Form I-20.

- Authorization is for one specific employer and for a specific period of time.

- Student must secure the training opportunity before CPT can be authorized.

- Student can have more than one CPT authorization at the same time. However, a separate CPT authorization is needed for each employer and each CPT segment.

- CPT requires a signed cooperative agreement or letter from the employer.

- One year of full-time CPT eliminates a student’s eligibility for OPT .

Optional Practical Training (OPT)
- Training relates directly to the student’s major area of study.

- Is not an integral part of a student’s established curriculum.

- Allows the student to work for any employer, as long as the training relates to the student’s major course of study

- Can occur before or after the student’s program end date.

- DSO recommends OPT in SEVIS.

- Authorized by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), and the student is issued an Employment Authorization Document (EAD).

- Student does not have to secure training before the DSO can recommend OPT.

- Periods of OPT cannot overlap.

- Students are eligible for an additional 12 months of OPT authorization, when they change to a higher educational level.

### Regulations and Information

Complex rules apply to CPT because the training opportunity must comply with both:

- Federal regulations

- School policies regarding internships, experiential learning, etc.

DSOs must understand federal regulations to determine if a student qualifies for CPT.

Regulations and resources applicable to CPT:

- 8 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) 214.2(f)(10) and (f)(10)(i)

- Practical Training page on ICE.gov

- August 2026 Broadcast Message: Guidance for Designated School Officials regarding Curricular Practical Training and Broadcast Message: Reminder of Liability for DSOs Regarding CPT Authorization

### Eligibility

A DSO can authorize CPT for an F-1 student enrolled at an SEVP-certified college, university, conservatory, or seminary if the student:

- Has been lawfully enrolled on a full-time basis for at least a full academic year. Note: An exception exists for students in graduate studies whose programs require earlier training.

- Is not enrolled in an English language training program.

- Has secured a training position.

- Will be participating in an established curriculum where practical training is integral and the training is directly related to the student’s major area of study.

CPT authorization is only required when the training is inside the United States. For example, a summer internship in a student’s home country does not need CPT authorization.

### CPT Process Overview

step Player Action

1 Student
- Requests CPT using the school’s established processes.

2 DSO
- Reviews request and determines student’s eligibility for CPT, including confirming that the student is participating in an established curriculum where practical training is integral and making a determination that the training is directly related to the student’s major area of study.

3 DSO
- If eligible, authorizes CPT in SEVIS for a specific employer.

- Prints and signs Form I-20 with CPT authorization.

4 Student
- Begins work on or after the CPT start date. Note: The student cannot start work prior to the start date. All work must end by the CPT end date.

##

As a DSO, when you sign or re-sign the Form I-20, “Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status,” to authorize CPT, you are certifying under penalty of perjury that all information is true and correct to the best of your knowledge. This includes confirming that CPT is required or otherwise integral to the student’s curriculum.

Improper CPT authorization, such as for non-integral purposes, may subject both the institution and individual DSO to administrative or legal consequences. This includes withdrawal of SEVP certification and potential penalties for providing false information or misrepresenting the nature of the training.

DSOs serve a critical role in upholding the integrity of the nation’s foreign student program and serve as important partners with the federal government. If you believe you are being asked, directed, coerced or otherwise pressured to make attestations you feel do not comply with regulations, we want to hear from you. The new SEVP tipline serves as a confidential avenue for you to relay these concerns for further review by SEVP and Homeland Security Investigations (HSI). Please contact SEVPFraud@hsi.dhs.gov .

To authorize curricular practical training:

1. Go to the Student Information page.

2. Click CPT Employment Authorization in the Employment/Training section of the links on the left-hand side of the page. The CPT Employment page opens.

3. Click New CPT Employment . The Add CPT Employment page opens.

4. Complete the following fields:

- Employment Start Date : Enter the date the CPT training is to begin. Format MM/DD/YYYY. This is a required field.

CPT Start Date must be on or after the date that CPT is authorized in SEVIS. Students cannot legally work before CPT is authorized in SEVIS.

- Employment End Date : Enter the date the CPT training will end. Format MM/DD/YYYY. This is a required field.

- Full Time/Part Time : Indicate whether the training is full or part time. This is a required field. Full Time : More than 20 hours per week Part Time : 20 hours of less per week

DSOs must authorize CPT either on a part-time or full-time basis, in accordance with the school policies. When school is in session, the student must maintain a full course of study even when CPT is authorized.

- Employer Name : Enter the name of the company or organizations where the training will take place. This is a required field.

- Click Add Employer Address : Enter the address where the training will take place in the Add Address modal. This is a required field. See the Address Standards article in the Help Hub.

- Explain how the employment is curricular : Describe how the training is an integral part of the established curriculum. These comments display on the Employment Information page. Completing this field is highly recommended. (250-character limit)

- Employment Remarks : Enter any optional remarks about the employment. These comments display on the Current Request/Authorizations page. (250-character limit)

- Student Remarks : Enter any remarks about the training. These remarks print on the Form I-20. They also display on the Student Information page until they are overwritten in the future. (1,000-character limit)

Note: SEVIS displays any comments previously entered for the student. You can choose to retain those comments, add new comments to them, or completely overwrite those comments.

5. Click one of the following: Add Employment , Reset Values , or Cancel .

- Add Employment : Adds the CPT authorization to the student record. An Update Successful page opens. Select either Return to Employment List or Print I-20 :

- Return to Employment List: Returns to the CPT Employment page.

- Print I-20: Prints the Form I-20 with the CPT authorization on page two.

- Students are expected to keep all Forms I-20 issued during their academic career.

- When information on the Form I-20 changes, it is best to give a new copy to the student.

- Find more information on allowable electronic signatures and Form I-20 transmission in SEVP’s policy guidance .

- Reset Values : Clears any values you entered.

- Cancel : Returns to the CPT Employment page without authorizing CPT.

##

DSOs can cancel curricular practical training before the CPT start date. After the CPT start date, the DSO can only edit the authorization to shorten the CPT end date.

To cancel CPT for a student:

1. Go to the CPT Employment page.

2. Click Cancel in the Command column for the CPT authorization you want to remove. The Cancel CPT Employment page opens.

3. Review the information. Click either Cancel Employment or Return .

- Cancel Employment : Cancels the CPT authorization. An Update Successful page opens. Click either Return to Employment List or Print I-20 : Return to Employment List: Opens the CPT Employment page. Print I-20: Prints the Form I-20.

- Students are expected to keep all Forms I-20 issued during their academic career.

- When information on the Form I-20 changes, it is best to give a new copy to the student.

- Find more information on allowable electronic signatures and Form I-20 transmission in SEVP’s policy guidance .

- Return : Takes you back to the CPT Employment page without canceling CPT.

Note: SEVIS continues to display canceled CPT authorizations. They are part of the history of actions taken on the student’s record.

##

DSOs can edit CPT authorizations to defer, update, or shorten the authorization. To edit CPT:

1. Go to the CPT Employment page.

2. Click Edit next to the CPT authorization you want to update. The Edit CPT Employment page opens.

3. Update the field(s) that needs to be edited.

4. Click Update Employment , Reset Values , or Cancel .

- Update Employment: Edits the CPT authorization in the student record. An Update Successful page opens. Select either Return to Employment List or Print I-20:

- Return to Employment List: Returns to the CPT Employment page.

- Print I-20: Prints the Form I-20 with the edited CPT authorization on page two.

- Students are expected to keep all Forms I-20 issued during their academic career.

- When information on the Form I-20 changes, it is best to give a new copy to the student.

- Find information on allowable electronic signatures and Form I-20 transmission in SEVP’s policy guidance Use of Electronic Signatures and Transmission for the Form I-20 .

- Reset Values: Clears any values you entered.

- Cancel : Returns to the CPT Employment page without authorizing CPT.

Any changes made to CPT authorization display in Event History.

##

DSOs can view CPT information in four different locations in the student’s record:

1. Student List: Students with Curricular Practical Training (CPT) : Accessed from Student Lists page.
2. Employment Information page: Accessed from Student Information page.
3. Current Requests/Authorization Details page: Accessed from Student Information page.
4. Event History page: Accessed from Student Information page.

## Student List: Students With Curricular Practical Training (CPT)

This student list allows DSOs to find students with active or future CPT authorizations quickly. A student with multiple CPT authorizations will appear on the list more than once.

## Employment Information Page

SEVIS displays CPT authorizations on the Employment Information page.

## Current Requests/Authorization Details Page

The Current Requests/Authorization Details page displays information on current and future CPT authorizations only.

## Student Event History

SEVIS captures and displays the history of CPT authorizations in the student’s Event History.
